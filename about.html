Who We Are

A Veteran Initiative Charting New Waters

Shellback Marines Pvt Ltd was born from a clear and pressing need — to transform the way nautical charts are procured and distributed in India. For too long, mariners have navigated a fragmented, slow, and paper-heavy system to obtain the very charts that keep them safe at sea.

We are a distinguished eCommerce platform specializing in hydrographic products and nautical charting solutions, built by maritime professionals for maritime professionals. Our comprehensive catalogue serves naval officers, merchant mariners, coastal fishermen, offshore industry operators, and maritime enthusiasts alike.

With an unwavering commitment to navigational excellence, we ensure that every chart we supply meets the highest standards of accuracy, currency, and compliance — because at sea, precision is not a preference, it is a necessity.

What Drives Us

Our Aim & Our Vision

Our Aim

Shellback Marines Pvt Ltd envisions a groundbreaking and pertinent improvement in the way nautical charts are procured and distributed across India. We aim to enhance the efficiency, accessibility, and accuracy of chart distribution for every class of mariner.

We are actively working toward fully digitizing the procurement process through our website and upcoming mobile app — creating a one-stop gateway for the browsing, selection, ordering, and secure payment for nautical charts on a single unified platform.

  • Digitize the end-to-end chart procurement workflow
  • Reduce lead times from weeks to hours
  • Ensure only corrected, up-to-date editions are supplied
  • Reshape distribution for the digital age of maritime navigation

Our Vision

We envision a seamless, expedited process for obtaining nautical charts — where mariners effortlessly browse, select, and acquire the charts they need with unparalleled efficiency. By leveraging cutting-edge technology, we eliminate the barriers that have long hindered swift access to critical navigational information.

Shellback Marines is committed to democratizing chart procurement — ensuring accessibility for mariners of all backgrounds, vessel types, and experience levels. Through our intuitive platform, we empower seafarers worldwide to navigate a comprehensive catalogue of charts, available at their fingertips, 24/7.
