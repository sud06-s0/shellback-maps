Safety at Sea

Accurate and up-to-date nautical charts provide essential information for safe navigation, helping mariners avoid hazards, shoals, and dangerous shallow waters in unfamiliar passages.

Hydrographic surveys offer detailed underwater topography, enabling precise route planning — reducing fuel consumption, cutting travel time, and lowering operational costs.

Resource Optimization

Chart Intelligence

Every Detail Matters
When Lives Are at Stake

Nautical charts are not merely maps — they are living documents constantly updated with Notice to Mariners, corrected for shifting sand bars, newly discovered wrecks, changed buoy positions, and updated depth soundings. At Shellback Marines, we stock only the latest corrected editions.

Whether you are navigating the shallow backwaters of Kerala, the busy shipping lanes of the Bay of Bengal, or the open waters of the Arabian Sea, our charts provide the granular detail that professional navigators rely on.

Depth Contours

Precise bathymetric soundings for safe passage through shallow and congested waterways.

Lights & Buoys

Complete light characteristics, buoy positions, and aid-to-navigation data for night sailing.

Current & Tides

Tidal stream data, current patterns, and seasonal monsoon routing information.

Hazard Marking

Wrecks, rocks, restricted areas, and maritime traffic separation schemes clearly marked.
