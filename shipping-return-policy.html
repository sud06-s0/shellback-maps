SECTION 02

Shipping & Delivery

Shellback Marines ships within India only and uses reputable courier companies to ensure your charts arrive safely and on time.

  • You will receive an email and/or text message with tracking details once your order has been dispatched.
  • We deliver to most parts of India within 3–7 business days.
  • You can reach us at abhisek@shellbackmarines.com or +91 9742172784 between Monday–Saturday, 11 AM – 7 PM for any queries.

While we use reputable couriers and work around the clock to ensure timely delivery, Shellback Marines cannot be held responsible for shipping delays due to natural disasters, strikes, bank holidays, or situations beyond our control.

SECTION 03

Standard Shipping — FREE
All orders shipped at no charge across India.

Express delivery charges, if applicable, will be calculated as per actuals and communicated to you prior to dispatch.

SECTION 04

Cancellations

Orders once placed cannot be cancelled or changed once they have been dispatched.

To cancel an order before dispatch, please call us on +91 9742172784 as soon as possible.

SECTION 05

Returns & Exchange

Due to hygiene and safety concerns, Shellback Marines operates a strict no-exchanges or refunds policy across the website.

This guarantees that every product you receive is:

Fresh
Original
Unopened
High Quality

This is an unparalleled commitment in an eCommerce landscape driven by returns and exchanges.

If there is a genuine case for exchange, please contact us at abhisek@shellbackmarines.com within 3 days of receiving your order. We handle these exchanges on a case-by-case basis.

SECTION 06

Damaged Products

In the event that a product reaches you in a damaged condition, please email us at abhisek@shellbackmarines.com within 3 working days of receipt.

  • Include clear images of the damaged product in your email.
  • Mention your order number and a brief description of the damage.
  • Our team will review your case and assist you as quickly as possible.
